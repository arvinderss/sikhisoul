# Sources

The assets were reconstructed from the scans and photographs listed below. None of those images are included in this
repository; only original vector reconstructions and tracings are. Dates and attributions are as given by the source
or its uploader, and have not been independently verified.

| Source | Used for | Holder / location |
|---|---|---|
| Burhanpuri "Sunehri" saroop, Sri Guru Granth Sahib, early 1700s (uploader's dating), 652 pages | 42 border designs, opening-spread illumination (pp.7–8), cloud heading (p.33) | Internet Archive `burhanpuri_saroop_gold`; photographs watermarked atinderpalsingh.com |
| Bhanno manuscript, 1700s, 1,204 pages | Opening frame (pp.70–71), covers (pp.2, 1203), index layout | Panjab Digital Library, M-3586 |
| MN-000403, Gurbaani Shabad | Cusped-arch opening study, index layouts | Gurmukhi Manuscripts at Kurukshetra University |
| Bhai Rupa pothi, 1600s, 524 pages | Framed Mool Mantar panel (p.488) | Bhai Rupa collection |
| S.S. Kapoor & M.K. Kapoor, *Epistles and Signets* | Nishans of Guru Arjan, Guru Hargobind, Guru Har Rai | Book reproductions of the Kartarpuri, Sabot and Dehra Dun Birs |
| Individual images (SikhiWiki / community uploads) | Nishans of Guru Hargobind, Guru Har Rai, Guru Tegh Bahadur, Guru Gobind Singh; red Mool Mantar; Nanakshahi rupee; hukamnama layouts | Various |
| Utara Damdami Bir; Janamsakhi Bhai Bala | Design studies (millefleur frame, triangular field) | Various |
