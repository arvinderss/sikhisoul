## What this means in practice

**You may:** use any asset in personal, religious, educational or commercial work. That includes printed and bound
gutkas, pothis, facsimiles, cards, textiles and software. You may also modify, recolour, combine and redistribute the assets.

**You must:**
1. **Credit the project.** For example:
   *"Ornament from the Sikh Manuscript Revival asset library, CC BY-SA 4.0, <repository URL>"*.
   In a printed book, a colophon line is enough.
2. **Share alike.** If you publish modified or derived versions of these assets, or new assets built from them,
   you must release them under CC BY-SA 4.0 (or a later compatible version). Do the same for the code under GPL-3.0-or-later.
3. **Add no restrictions.** You may not add legal terms or technical measures (DRM, exclusive-rights claims)
   that stop others from exercising these same freedoms.

A printed book that only *uses* the ornament does not, by itself, have to release its whole text under this licence.
The assets you used, and any changes you made to them, stay CC BY-SA.

## Patents, design registrations and prior art

- These designs are reconstructions of ornament and calligraphy from manuscripts made in the 16th–18th centuries,
  which have long been in the public domain. Publishing them openly, with a dated record, makes them **prior art**.
  See [PRIOR_ART.md](PRIOR_ART.md).
- The GPL-3.0 includes an express patent licence and a ban on patent aggression for the code.
- CC BY-SA 4.0 does not grant or waive patent or design-registration rights, but its share-alike and no-added-restrictions terms
  bind everyone who redistributes the work. If anyone tries to register or patent these designs, the dated public record
  in this repository is the evidence to challenge them.

## Underlying sources

The original manuscripts are public-domain cultural heritage. The **photographs and scans** of them belong to their holders
(Panjab Digital Library, Kurukshetra University, private collections and photographers). This repository therefore contains
**no source photographs or scans**, only original vector reconstructions and line tracings. See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
