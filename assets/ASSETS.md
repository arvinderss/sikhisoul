# Asset catalogue

All files are scalable SVG (viewBox set), editable in Illustrator, Inkscape, Affinity or Figma.
Colours are flat fills sampled from the scans and cleaned up, so you can recolour them freely.

## 01_borders_burhanpuri (42 border styles)
Taken from the Burhanpuri "Sunehri" saroop (early 1700s by uploader's dating). The manuscript
changes its border design section by section, and these are the distinct designs found across all 652 pages.
- `tiles/` one seamless repeat unit per style. Use it as a pattern swatch.
- `strips/` a ready-made run of the border, about 560 px long.
- `motifs/` the single flower or bud from 12 styles, cut out on its own.
- `index.json` style key, title and source page.
Keys `bur_a**` are gold cartouche bands; keys `bur_b**` are floral bands.

## 02_opening_illumination
- `bha_*` Bhanno MS (Panjab Digital Library) opening folio: scalloped *kangura* band with finials,
  oval cartouche band on lapis, and a full page-frame reconstruction (`bha_opening_frame_full.svg`).
- `bur_*` Burhanpuri opening spread (pp.7–8): lapis panels with stepped cartouches (horizontal and vertical),
  lobed mandorla in a cusped niche, ogee arcade headpiece, 8-point star band, crimson diamond lattice,
  outer scroll-vine border, cloud-band heading (placeholder text), baluster columns.

## 03_calligraphy_nishans
Line-traced from the scans in your folder. They reproduce historical handwriting exactly as written.
Attributions follow the captions in the sources (Kapoor, *Epistles and Signets*; SikhiWiki/Folkscanomy uploads).
- Nishans of Guru Arjan (Kartarpuri Bir; Sabot Bir), Guru Hargobind (two), Guru Har Rai (two, incl. Dehra Dun Bir 1659),
  Guru Tegh Bahadur, Guru Gobind Singh; Mool Mantar attributed to Guru Arjan (red ink); a framed Mool Mantar panel
  from the Bhai Rupa pothi; writing attributed to Guru Gobind Singh.
- Not traced: the signature, the hukamnama Nishans and the Banda Singh seal. Those source images are too small
  (about 200–400 px) to trace faithfully. If you find larger scans, they can be added.

## 04_binding_coins
- `bind_bhanno_front_arabesque.svg` the silver-tooled arabesque from the Bhanno front cover, traced as it is.
- `bind_bhanno_arabesque_symmetric.svg` the same design made four-way symmetric, ready to send for a brass block,
  laser-engraved stamp or foil die.
- `coin_ranjit_singh_*` both faces of a Nanakshahi rupee (Ranjit Singh era).

## 05_source_studies
Simplified vector studies of eight designs: the sawtooth edge, the Kurukshetra cusped arch, the Burhanpuri Nishan niche,
the lapis arabesque frame, the cloud-collar cartouche, the Damdami flower-filled frame, the Janamsakhi triangular field
and the two hukamnama layouts.
