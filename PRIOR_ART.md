# Defensive publication / prior-art statement

**First published:** 25 October 2026 (IST), the date of the first commit in this repository.

The Sikh Manuscript Revival contributors publish the designs in `assets/` openly and for free, so that they become part
of the public record. This publication is meant to stop anyone from claiming them as new, whether by patent, by
industrial-design or design registration, or by any other exclusive right.

## What is disclosed

1. **42 repeating border designs** (`assets/01_borders_burhanpuri/`), recorded from the Burhanpuri "Sunehri"
   saroop of Sri Guru Granth Sahib. The uploader dates it to the early 1700s. Each design is given as a tile, a strip and
   (for 12 designs) a single motif, and the source page is recorded in `index.json`.
2. **Opening-folio illumination** (`assets/02_opening_illumination/`): the scalloped *kangura* band with finials,
   the oval cartouche band and a full page frame from the 1700s Bhanno manuscript (Panjab Digital Library); and
   lapis panels with stepped cartouches, a lobed mandorla in a cusped niche, an ogee arcade headpiece, an 8-point star band,
   a diamond lattice, a scroll-vine border, a cloud-band heading and baluster columns from the Burhanpuri saroop.
3. **Calligraphy** (`assets/03_calligraphy_nishans/`): line tracings of the Nishans and Mool Mantar
   attributed to the Sikh Gurus, as reproduced in the sources listed in SOURCES.md.
4. **Binding tooling** (`assets/04_binding_coins/`): the silver-tooled arabesque from the Bhanno manuscript cover,
   both as found and as a four-fold symmetric panel, plus both faces of a Nanakshahi rupee.
5. **Design studies** (`assets/05_source_studies/`) and the generator code in `tools/`. Running the code
   reproduces the border and illumination files exactly.

## Integrity

`MANIFEST.sha256` lists a SHA-256 hash for every published file. Together with the Git history (and any later release
tags or archives, for example on Software Heritage or Zenodo), it fixes what was disclosed and when.

## Notes

- The ornament, calligraphy and binding designs come from public-domain manuscripts made between the 16th and 18th
  centuries. This repository records them; it does not claim them as new inventions.
- Anyone may use them under [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). No one may claim exclusive rights in them.
